# Infra Skills Test — Candidate Brief

Hi! To help you prepare, here’s what our hands‑on, infra interview looks like.

---

## What this is

A 30‑minute, scenario‑based troubleshooting session where **you guide us** to diagnose and fix an issue. Think of it as being the on‑call Infra Engineer while we act as the person with keyboard access.

**Goal:** see how you approach real problems: your debugging flow, communication, and practical know‑how.

---

## What you’ll do

* Ask us to run commands, open configs, or check dashboards (e.g., Docker, Nginx, DNS, CI/CD).
* Interpret the outputs we share and tell us the next step.
* Explain your reasoning as you go.
* Propose a quick fix and a follow‑up improvement.

> You won’t need to deploy anything on your machine (no local setup required).

---

## What we provide during the session

* A realistic incident story.
* A facilitator (us) who will “be your hands” on the system.

---

## Time breakdown (approx.)

* 3–5 min — Context & scope
* 20–22 min — Guided troubleshooting & fix
* 3–5 min — Short retro & what you’d improve next

---

## What we’re looking for (evaluation)

* **Troubleshooting flow:** do you start broad, isolate layers (network → proxy → app → DB → CI/CD)?
* **Communication:** clear, step‑by‑step requests
* **Tool fluency:** Linux basics, logs, Docker, reverse proxy, DNS, CI/CD reading.
* **Ops hygiene:** safe fixes, rollback mindset, secret handling awareness.

This is **not** a memory test; it’s about your method.

---

## What to prepare (recommended)

* Be ready to **talk through** your approach out loud.
* Refresh common commands:

  * Networking
  * Linux/Docker
  * Reverse proxy concepts
* Have a notepad to outline your decision tree.

---

## What’s not required

* No local coding or laptop setup
* No perfect recall of flags/syntax
* No cloud account access

---

## Logistics

* Duration: \~30 minutes (video call)
* Format: play‑along troubleshooting; we share outputs as requested

Looking forward to meeting you!
